**Will it share my existing posts?**
No. An administrator can share an older entry by hand with Share now in its editor panel. Republishing never sends an entry again.

**Why was a channel skipped?**
The editor panel and the Buffer Plus page say why: the network needs an image the entry lacks, Instagram cannot take the image's shape, the channel is disconnected or locked, no Pinterest board is chosen, the text is too long even without the description, or Buffer's API cannot post there (YouTube, WhatsApp, Start Page).

**Which tags work in post text?**
{title}, {description} (the entry's Description or SEO description, also written {excerpt}) and {url}.

**Why does a figure say "None yet"?**
Buffer reports figures about once a day, and only those a network gives. Missing is not zero.

**Do impressions include posts made elsewhere?**
Yes. They cover every post on the shared channels, split into posts made directly on the network and through Buffer, this plugin included. Sent, queued and failed count this plugin's posts only.

**Why only 31 days of figures?**
Buffer limits how far back figures go by plan: the Free plan gives 31 days (Buffer's own answer, not in its docs). The plugin learns the limit from Buffer and shows what the plan allows.

**Can a post go out twice?**
No: after an uncertain answer it looks for the post in Buffer before sending again.

**How many Buffer requests does it make?**
About 40 to 50 a day for 10 channels, from Buffer's per-account limit shared with your other keys and assistants (Free plan: 250 a day, 3,000 in 30 days). Reports pause while less than "Leave for other tools" is left.

**What if Buffer's limit is reached?**
Posts are not lost. They wait and go out by themselves when the limit refills. Buffer's separate per-channel posting limit can refuse a post: select Retry the next day.

**Does it work on every network?**
It follows Buffer's documented rules but is not yet tried live on every network. Please report what you find.
